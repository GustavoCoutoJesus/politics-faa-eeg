Participant Tracking Data Description
======================================

Note: The datasets described here can be found in:

- Statements & Faces 
  https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds007688/versions/1.0.0

- Dynamic Faces - combine eeg_biosemi and eeg_biosemi_pilot
  https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30971284.

Participant Counts:
-------------------
- StatementsFaces (Sourcedata): 44 participants
- StatementsFaces (Processed): 44 participants
- DynamicFaces (Sourcedata): 62 participants
- DynamicFaces (Processed): 55 participants

Directory Structure & Logic:
- The data generation scripts rely on a standard BIDS (Brain Imaging Data Structure) directory layout.
- Sourcedata: In the 'sourcedata' directory, the folders are prefixed with 'sub-'. This identifies all participants initially collected.
- Processed: After being successfully preprocessed, usable data can be found in 'derivatives/preprocessed' batch directory. 
- Output: The accompanying CSV maps the participant attrition across these stages. However, the processed recordings are not necessarily ideal for analysis, and must still be filtered after a quality check.
